# ChatGPT Parity Scorecard

This scorecard is the fixed north star for the experimental ChatGPT-parity branch.

Target: **90 / 100 or higher** before signoff.

## Metrics

1. Sidebar structure and density — 15 points
   - Dark vertical rail
   - Compact top controls
   - Scrollable session list
   - Session row density close to ChatGPT

2. Top bar proportion and placement — 10 points
   - Slim top chrome
   - Left-aligned model/app label
   - Right-aligned utility controls

3. Main canvas color and surface treatment — 10 points
   - Dark neutral background
   - Low-noise surfaces
   - Avoid glossy / heavy glass treatment

4. Transcript column geometry — 15 points
   - Readable centered conversation width
   - Similar horizontal margins and vertical spacing
   - Messages positioned like ChatGPT rather than dashboard cards

5. Message styling parity — 10 points
   - Assistant replies visually low-chrome
   - User replies in compact rounded accent bubbles
   - Similar spacing and rhythm between turns

6. Composer parity — 15 points
   - Compact composer height
   - Bottom-centered placement
   - Single-line default with auto-grow
   - Send control proportions close to ChatGPT

7. Typography parity — 10 points
   - Similar hierarchy between chrome, session list, and messages
   - Similar muted-vs-primary contrast balance

8. Interaction parity — 10 points
   - New chat affordance placement
   - Session switching feel
   - Scroll behavior
   - Focus states and hover states

9. Product-specific control restraint — 5 points
   - Training controls remain present but do not overwhelm the ChatGPT-like structure

## Scoring Method

- Score each category independently from `0` to its maximum.
- Sum to a total out of `100`.
- Convert to a 10-point score by dividing by `10`.
- Experimental branch target: **9.0 / 10 or better**.
